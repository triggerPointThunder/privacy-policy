Information We Collect

We collect different types of information when you use our App, which may include:

1. Automatically Collected Information

When you use our App, some basic information is collected automatically to help the App run smoothly and improve your overall experience. This may include your device type, operating system version, language settings, time of access, and usage details (such as which features are most used). This helps us improve reliability, functionality, and user experience.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our app may use small data files or similar tracking technologies to improve your experience. These help us understand feature usage, remember preferences, and improve app performance. You may disable such features in your settings, but some functionality may be affected.

3. Content, Metadata, and Precise Location

When you add photos or videos in the App, they may include metadata such as date/time, text, signatures, or location. If you enable location features, the App collects your device’s precise GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) to overlay on photos or videos. This data is used solely for this purpose and is not shared with third parties except as required by law. You can control whether this metadata is included in your content through the App’s settings.
